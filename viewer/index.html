Stability is a rough estimate. Masses: electronics, switches and keycaps 60 g, printed case 30 g, printed parts at nylon density, steel at 7.85 g/cm³. "Tips" is the force on the worst-placed key that would roll the half inward over its innermost support, with no laptop case. "Slides" assumes silicone feet (friction 0.8). For scale: Choc switches actuate at 35–60 gf, and a firm keystroke peaks at roughly 100–300 gf.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Hinges swing freely on steel pins; the angle comes from a fixed triangle and a hard stop, so nothing relies on friction and nothing needs tightening.
